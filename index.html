Selected work

  1. 01 2026

    Warroom

    A realtime decision room. The meeting is the artifact.

    Multi-user boards where imported documents become live cards, decisions are made with one-vote polls the database enforces, and a facilitator can freeze the room at the server boundary. Every room ends with an AI-written dispatch of what was decided, with evidence and vote counts. Free and Pro tiers gate server-side through live Stripe checkout.

    • Hono
    • Cloudflare Durable Objects
    • Stripe
    • Claude
    • Playwright

    Mira, Theo, Ada, and Jules each teach differently. Real accounts, notes and flashcards in Postgres, and an agent tool loop that can read and write the desk. Self-hosted on Vercel and Neon with authentication the app owns.

  3. 03 2026

    Medicare Fraud Detection

    Flagging high-risk providers from public CMS billing data.

    Joins Medicare Part D prescriber data for Alabama, Georgia, and Tennessee with the OIG exclusion list as ground truth, engineers provider-level features, and trains an XGBoost classifier. A Streamlit app lets you investigate individual providers with SHAP explanations.

    • Python
    • XGBoost
    • Pandas
    • SHAP
    • Streamlit

    Source

  4. 04 2025

    Last-Mile Delivery Engine

    Event-driven logistics backend in Go.

    Ingests orders through Kafka, finds shoppers within 15 km using Redis Geo, and pushes offers over WebSockets. Order claiming is atomic through row-level locking in Postgres, so two couriers can never take the same job. A ghost-car simulator exercises the whole loop locally.

    • Go
    • Kafka
    • Redis
    • PostgreSQL
    • WebSockets
    • Docker

    Source Demo video

  5. 05 2025

    Wallet Guard

    A budgeting app that intervenes before you overspend. Built at VandyHacks 2025.

    Walk into a high-spend zone and it calls your phone with a coaching voice grounded in your real transactions. Savings commitments are escrowed on Solana for 21 days, so you cannot quietly back out. Ships with a color-blind mode, large text, and full Spanish localization.

    • React
    • Gemini
    • ElevenLabs
    • Twilio
    • Solana
    • Mapbox

    Source

  6. 06 2025

    Alabama Flood Map

    Weather, river gauges, and social vulnerability on one map.

    Pulls ten-day precipitation forecasts, NOAA river-level forecasts, live USGS gauge readings, and CDC vulnerability data by census tract, then lets you ask about flood risk in plain language with an assistant that reads the same data.

    • Node.js
    • Mapbox GL
    • NOAA / USGS
    • PostgreSQL
    • OpenAI
